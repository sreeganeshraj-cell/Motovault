# MotoVault

## Vision
MotoVault is a premium vehicle care and storage service designed for motor enthusiasts who want their vehicles to be maintained with the same passion and care as they would do themselves.

## Concept
A secure, clean, and professionally managed garage where customers can store their cars and motorcycles while receiving regular care, maintenance activity, and transparent updates.

## Target Users
- Motor enthusiasts (bike/car lovers)
- Owners with multiple vehicles
- Professionals with limited time
- NRIs or people away from home

## Problem
Vehicle owners struggle to:
- Maintain vehicles regularly
- Trust others to handle their vehicles properly
- Ensure vehicles are kept in good condition when unused

## Solution
MotoVault provides:
- Secure storage with fixed capacity
- Routine care (cleaning, engine start, riding)
- Transparent weekly reports with photos/videos
- Premium concierge services for enthusiasts

## Core Value
"Your vehicle is treated like it’s ours."
